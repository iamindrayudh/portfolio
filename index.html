About Me

I am Indrayudh Mandal, an ML researcher at Deccan AI and a speech NLP researcher at Lingo Labs, IIT Gandhinagar, where I work under the guidance of Prof. Mayank Singh.

My work spans audio models, Automatic Speech Recognition for Indic languages, text-to-speech evaluation, and evaluation research for language and vision-language systems, with an emphasis on building robust pipelines that turn research ideas into usable tools.
